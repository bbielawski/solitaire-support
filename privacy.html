Privacy Policy

Solitaire: Klondike Classic  ·  Effective July 22, 2026

The short version

We don't collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data — about you or anyone else. There's no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no third-party trackers, and no account or sign-in of any kind. Everything the app remembers (your game in progress, win/loss stats, chosen theme, purchase status) stays only on your own device.

What we don't collect

  • No name, email, or contact information
  • No location data
  • No advertising identifiers or tracking
  • No usage analytics or crash reporting sent to us
  • No data shared with third parties, because none is collected in the first place

What's stored on your device

The app saves your current game, win/loss stats, and settings locally on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults). This information never leaves your device and is not visible to us. Uninstalling the app removes it.

In-app purchases

The one-time full-game unlock is processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. We never see or handle your payment details — that transaction is governed by Apple's own Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The app is rated for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future update adds optional analytics — we'll update this page and the "Effective" date above before that update is released.
