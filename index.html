Common questions

How does the free trial work?
You can play 5 full games for free. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited play — forever, with no subscription and no ads.
I bought the full unlock but I'm on a new device. How do I get it back?
Open Settings inside the app and tap "Restore Purchases." As long as you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it, your purchase is restored automatically at no extra cost.
Does the app collect any of my data?
No. The app doesn't collect, track, or share any personal data. Your stats and progress are stored only on your own device. See our Privacy Policy for details.
How do I unlock new card back and felt themes?
Themes unlock automatically as you win games — nothing to buy. Check the Theme section in Settings to see how many wins each one needs.
I found a bug or the app crashed. What should I do?
Email us at the address above with a quick description of what happened — the more detail, the faster we can fix it.
